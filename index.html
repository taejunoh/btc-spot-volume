All data comes straight from each exchange's public API in your browser and refreshes every 60 seconds. Volume is summed in the base asset (BTC), so USDT, USD, USDC, FDUSD, EUR and KRW markets add up directly.

USD value is each candle's BTC volume × the Binance BTC/USDT close. The rightmost candle is still in progress.

Gate and HTX have faced persistent allegations of inflating volume through wash trading, so they're marked Flagged and left out of the default Trusted exchanges preset. Share over time uses a rolling 24-hour sum (7 days on the daily chart) instead of single candles to cut noise.

HTX daily candles close on UTC+8 and Bithumb's on KST, so they're a few hours off from the other exchanges' UTC days. Some exchanges (such as Bybit) may be blocked depending on your region; failed exchanges drop out of the total and show in the Status column.
